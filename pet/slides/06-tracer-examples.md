<p class="takeaway">The radioactive atom is built into the molecule itself.</p>

Note:
FDG — 2-deoxy-2-[18F]fluoro-D-glucose. It is glucose with one change: the hydroxyl
group on carbon 2 is replaced by fluorine-18. That single swap is why it gets trapped
in cells, as we will see shortly.

PiB — Pittsburgh compound B. A benzothiazole that binds beta-amyloid plaques. The
carbon-11 sits in the N-methyl group; because it is a carbon, the labelled molecule is
chemically identical to the unlabelled one. Mostly a research tracer because of the
20-minute half-life.

Florbetapir (Amyvid) — an amyloid tracer labelled with fluorine-18 at the end of the
ethoxy chain, so it can be produced centrally and shipped. It is one of the
FDA-approved amyloid PET tracers, along with florbetaben and flutemetamol.

The structures are drawn from their SMILES strings; the isotope labels mark where the
radioactive atom sits.
