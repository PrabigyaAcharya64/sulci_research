<p class="takeaway">The axis is in place, but the frame can still turn around it.</p>

Note:
Precession aligned one axis. That removes two degrees of freedom, not three — the
whole coordinate frame can still spin about the axis we just fixed, and every one
of those spins is a different rotation. A rotation in three dimensions needs three
numbers, and so far we have spent only two. This is exactly why the paper needs a
second rotation, and it is the cleanest way to motivate it.
