<p class="takeaway">Every location moves identically. Brains are not identical.</p>

Note:
If the three numbers are constants, then every vertex receives the same matrix and
the whole sphere turns as one rigid body. That is genuinely useful — it is the
global alignment step — but it cannot do the real work. The paper says plainly that
after the rigid rotation the corresponding locations are still not all aligned.
Some sulci line up, many local differences remain, and no single rotation can fix
them, because the subjects genuinely differ in shape.
