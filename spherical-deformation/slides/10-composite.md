<p class="ref">Eq. (9) — Theorem 1: this reaches any R ∈ SO(3)</p>
<div class="eq sm" data-tex="\underbrace{c_{u_1},\ c_{u_2}}_{\text{where the axis goes}}\qquad\underbrace{\omega}_{\text{spin about it}}"></div>
<p class="takeaway">A whole rotation, written with exactly three numbers.</p>

Note:
Here is the pay-off of the construction. Any rotation of the sphere — three degrees
of freedom — is now encoded by three ordinary scalars. Two of them say where the
axis travels in the flat tangent plane, the third says how far to spin about it.
None of the three depends on a polar coordinate system, and none of them blows up
at a pole. Read the product right to left: precession first, then the intrinsic
spin about the axis precession produced.
