Note:
One more angle, taken about the axis we have just aligned, and the frame is fully
determined. Two successive rotations — of the axis, then about the axis — and the
paper's Theorem 1 states these are sufficient to represent any rotation in SO(3),
for any choice of reference axis z. That last clause matters: because it holds for
any z, the paper does not have to commit to a special reference axis.
